# JobCheck Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 7, 2026

JobCheck processes job messages, sender email addresses and listing URLs locally in the user's browser to identify common warning signals. The extension does not transmit submitted content to JobCheck, the developer or an external analysis service.

JobCheck stores a report in browser storage only when the user explicitly selects the save option. Saved reports can include the submitted material, remain on that device and can be deleted by clearing the saved history. Website and extension storage are separate.

The extension uses the `contextMenus` permission solely to let a user explicitly send selected webpage text to JobCheck's local extension page. It does not monitor browsing activity or collect webpage content automatically.

JobCheck does not sell personal data, use submitted content for advertising, or share submitted content with third parties. Links opened by the user, including the public JobCheck website, are governed by the destination site's own privacy practices.

For privacy questions or requests, use the contact method listed on the project's public GitHub repository.
